Questions

Before you come in.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome during office hours. If you want to be sure someone is free, send a WhatsApp and we'll hold a time for you.

Are you lawyers?

No. We are not attorneys and cannot give legal advice or represent you before immigration authorities. We help you prepare, translate and organize documents, and we refer you to a licensed immigration attorney whenever your case needs one.

What should I bring to the first visit?

An ID, any letters or forms you received, and whatever records you have — bank statements, receipts, last year's tax return. If you have nothing organized, that's fine. That's why we're here.

How much does it cost?

The first conversation is free. After that, we give you a written price before starting any work. Monthly bookkeeping plans are available for small businesses.

Can you help me if I don't have a Social Security number?

Yes. We can help you apply for an ITIN, file taxes and open a business with the documents you have.
